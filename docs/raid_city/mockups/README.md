# Mockups (layout reference only)

Concept art Michael approved as a layout reference (Nov 19 2026). Labels, scale and building counts are rough.
The design doc's numbers (sizes, distances, road widths) take priority over the images.

| File | Shows | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `region_map.jpg` | Whole region: small town (north), countryside, mountain statue, waterfall, big city (south) | Region layout, extract and POI placement |
| `city_topdown.jpg` | "Nova Reach City": river, 3 bridges, Downtown, Neon Market, Industrial Docks, Suburbs, police, hospital | District layout and the first district slice |
| `downtown_aerial.jpg` | Central Bank, downtown towers, suspension bridge, flying-car traffic | Bank massing, air lanes, landmark scale |
| `street_view.jpg` | Neon Market street at night: grocery, ATM, taxi, scooter, hover car | Street kit, props, lighting targets |

The mockup's three generic extracts (North, East, South) are replaced by the typed civilian/criminal extracts in
`../01_rules_and_loop.md` and placed in `../02_world_layout.md`.

`docs/raid_city/.gdignore` keeps Godot from importing these images into the game.
